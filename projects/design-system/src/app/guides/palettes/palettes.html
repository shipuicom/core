Palettes

Every colour in Ship is a twelve-step scale exposed as custom properties. The built-in scales are hand-tuned for light and dark mode, and you can add your own, which every skin then supports as a colour class.

The scales

Each palette emits --<name>-1 to --<name>-12, two gradients -g2 / -g3, and -c8, a text colour with enough contrast on step 8. These are live tokens: toggle the theme to see the dark steps.

Using the steps

Ship's own skins lean on step 8 for a solid colour, with -c8 for the text on it and -g2 / -g3 for the raised gradient. Steps 1–4 give tinted surfaces and 6–7 borders. The same split works well in your own styles:

  • 1–4: tinted backgrounds, from subtle to pressed.
  • 6–7: borders and separators.
  • 8–9: solid fills; 9 for hover.
  • 10–12: coloured text and icons on light surfaces, from lower to higher contrast.

Colour classes

primary, accent, warn, error and success are colour classes on every skin, usable as a color input or a plain class. base is the neutral grey scale the UI itself is built from.

Add your own palette

$shipPalettes adds scales to the global stylesheet. The shorthand is the hue, saturation and lightness of step 8; Ship generates the other steps and the gradients with the same curve as the theme editor.

Emit fewer steps

$shipPaletteSteps limits which numbered steps the colour palettes emit. The grey base scale is always emitted in full, because the stylesheet and every skin read it.
