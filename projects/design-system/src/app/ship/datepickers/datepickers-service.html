ShipCalendarService

ShipCalendarService is the signal-based calendar engine behind sh-datepicker. It is not provided in root — the datepicker provides its own instance, and you can do the same to build fully custom calendar UIs (booking grids, availability views, heatmaps) on top of the exact date math the datepicker uses.

Providing and configuring

Add the service to a component's providers and configure it through its writable signals: currentDate, monthsToShow, startOfWeek, weekdayLabels and locale.

Rendering the grid

The months() computed returns one ShipCalendarMonth per visible month, where dates is a 7-column grid padded with leading/trailing days from the adjacent months. weekdays() gives matching column headers rotated to your startOfWeek.
