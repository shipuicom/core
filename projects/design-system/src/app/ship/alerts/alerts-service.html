ShipAlertService

ShipAlertService is provided in root and is the imperative way to show toast alerts from anywhere — components, interceptors, effects. Alerts are queued in a reactive history, animate in, and auto-hide after a short timeout.

Try it:

Shorthand methods

success(), error(), warning(), info() and question() each show a single-line alert of the matching type.

Full control with addAlert

Use addAlert() when you need a body text or want to choose the type dynamically:

Alert history

Every alert is kept in the alertHistory signal (newest first). The alertHistoryIsOpen and alertHistoryIsHidden signals control the history panel, and removeAlert() / hideAlert() manage individual entries.
