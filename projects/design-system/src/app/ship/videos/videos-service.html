ShipVideoState

ShipVideoState is the centralized player store. sh-video provides its own instance by default, but you can provide it on a wrapper component to share a single store between the player, custom controls and editor chrome. The store itself stays passive — commands delegate to whichever player registered via attachPlayer().

Signals as the API

The writable signals are the control surface: setting playing plays, setting currentTime seeks, setting quality switches renditions. Observed signals like duration, bufferedRanges, levels or isLive are kept current by the player engine.
