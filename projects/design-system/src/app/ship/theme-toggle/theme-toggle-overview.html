Overview

<ship-theme-toggle> is a ready-made button that cycles the application between light , dark , and system appearance. It renders a Ship button with a matching icon and delegates the actual theme switching to the ShipThemeState service.

Usage

Drop the component anywhere — typically in a header or toolbar. It works out of the box with no inputs.

Styling

The toggle forwards color , variant , and size to its underlying shButton , so it matches the rest of your buttons.

Controlling the theme directly

The ShipThemeState service (provided in root) exposes a reactive theme signal plus toggleTheme() and setTheme() . Inject it to read or set the theme from your own UI.
