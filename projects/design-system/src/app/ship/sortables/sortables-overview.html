Apply the shSortable directive to a list, grid, or tree container to enable interactive drag-and-drop reordering.

It works the same way with a mouse, a finger and the keyboard. A mouse drag starts straight away. On touch, hold an item still for a moment to pick it up; a touch that moves first scrolls the page as usual. With a [sort-handle] focused, the arrow keys and Home/End move the item.
