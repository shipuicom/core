ShipSortableService

ShipSortableService is the root-provided drag registry shared by every ShipSortable container. It is what makes cross-container drops work: all sortable instances register themselves in activeInstances, and during a drag the service tracks the source container (activeSource), the dragged element (activeDraggedElement) and the container currently under the pointer (activeTarget).
