Slot it in

Blocks are the sections of a website, from @ship-ui/core/ship-block . Like the layouts they arrange what you put in them: a <sh-bl-hero> takes an eyebrow or sh-chip , an h1 , a p , buttons, a small and an img , and lays them out. Section blocks ( features , steps , stats , testimonials , pricing , faq , team , posts ) share one header: an element marked eyebrow , an h2 , a p and buttons marked actions , followed by their items ( sh-bl-feature , sh-bl-step , sh-bl-stat , sh-bl-testimonial , sh-bl-pricing-tier , details , sh-bl-member , sh-bl-post ). The header is centered; add class start to left-align it. The site chrome is <sh-bl-banner> , <sh-bl-header> (logo, nav , actions; collapses into a menu when narrow) and <sh-bl-footer> (one link column per nav ). Wrap the header in a <header> and the footer in a <footer> for the landmarks.

Responsive by container

Every block is a size container and reflows at its own width (960px and 640px), not the viewport's, so a block looks the same in a full page, a preview or a sidebar. Content is centered at --block-mw (1200px) with --block-py / --block-px around it; set those once to change every block, or a block's own --hero-mw , --features-py , … for one. Add class full for no max width or flush for no outer padding.

Variants

Every block takes a variant and a project-wide default can be set in ShipConfig ( {{ "{ blockHeader: { variant: 'type-c' } }" }} ). Blocks with an accent (eyebrow, icons, panels) also take a color . sh-bl-banner : tinted strip, type-b solid, type-c floating pill. sh-bl-header : nav after the logo, type-b nav centered, type-c floating rounded bar. sh-bl-hero : centered with the media below, type-b split, type-c cover (media behind the text). sh-bl-logos : centered row, type-b bordered grid, type-c caption beside the logos. sh-bl-features : open grid, type-b cards, type-c header beside a two-column list. sh-bl-split : 50/50 framed media, type-b media on a tinted panel, type-c media bleeding to the edge (class reverse puts the media first). sh-bl-steps : numbered row joined by a line, type-b vertical timeline, type-c numbered cards. sh-bl-stats : numbers split by hairlines, type-b cards, type-c solid colour panel. sh-bl-testimonials : card grid, type-b one large quote, type-c masonry. sh-bl-pricing : cards, type-b joined columns, type-c compact rows; mark a tier featured . sh-bl-faq : divided list, type-b header in a sticky column, type-c cards. sh-bl-cta : tinted panel, type-b solid panel, type-c one bordered row. sh-bl-newsletter : centered, type-b text beside the form, type-c sign-up card. sh-bl-team : round photos, type-b photo cards, type-c compact list. sh-bl-posts : card grid, type-b list, type-c featured first post. sh-bl-contact : details beside the form, type-b centered, type-c form card on a tinted band. sh-bl-footer : brand and link columns, type-b centered, type-c compact single row.
